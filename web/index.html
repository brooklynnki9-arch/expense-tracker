What it does

Record income and expenses

Add your pay and every purchase with its date.

Sort your spending

Put each expense in Food, Rent, Transport, or Other.

Set budgets

Pick a monthly limit for each category and get a warning when you go over.

See what's left

Monthly and all-time reports show your totals and where most of your money went.

How to start

  1. Open the tracker and add the money you earned this month as income.
  2. Add each thing you buy as an expense and pick a category for it.
  3. Set a budget for each category, then check the report to see how much you have left.

Your data is saved in your own browser only. Nobody else can see it, and you don't need an account.
